# sondalab-site art redesign (2026-11-12): owner picked six references (cursor.com, teenage.engineering, nousresearch.com, oxide.computer, ghostty.org, poolside.ai) and approved the design position: one art language (procedural 2px prints from art-core.js), remove CRT scenes, glow field and blurred card bars, art as hero subject, slug-seeded covers for posts and products, at most one full-bleed band per page, no art under text or beside a headline. Three directions are built in sondalab-site worktrees dir-a-plate (port 4341, vivid framed plates), dir-b-one-ink (4342, duotone ink plus accent), dir-c-exhibit (4343, dark-first numbered prints), each with a kit worktree site-dir-* in sondalab-ui; owner picks one after a critic pass. This supersedes the 2026-11-11 note that said no art in heads: the owner likes the posters as craft, the failure was placement in existing slots.
