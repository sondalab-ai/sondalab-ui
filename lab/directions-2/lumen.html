Lumen

Panes are islands of light on a darker frame, and depth comes from lightness, never from a line.
The pane that holds your attention is lit: an accent seam and the light it casts. Nothing else is.
Geist carries the voice in sentence case, and the accent is spent only where attention is.

Signature details

  1. The lit paneThe pane that has focus is the one lit. A 2px seam of the accent runs along its top edge, its light falls about 140px into the pane, its rim takes the accent at 46%, and its fill is one step lighter than the other panes. At a third of full size (the compare column) the focused pane is still the one you point at; the other panes stay unlit.
  2. The raised tileSelection lifts the row out of the island as an object: a lighter fill, a lit top hairline, a short cast, and the icon in the accent. Hover stays a flat wash; only selection lifts. Tabs, segmented items and the settings nav use the same tile.
  3. KeycapsEvery shortcut is a small physical key: 20px, r5, with an inset base. Inside a button the key rides along as a cap one lightness step off the fill. The step's direction comes from the fill's own lightness, so the cap reads on amber, on indigo #4c58e9 and on cyan.
  4. The glow ringFocus is the owner's flush ring (the border is the ring, with no gap), and an accent glow continues outward from it, 5px at 22%. It is contiguous with the ring, so no line of a second colour sits between.
  5. Status seamsToasts and terminal blocks carry their status in the same seam language: green when done, red when failed. The toast stack shows the next toast waiting behind it (scale .94, −11px).
  6. The status dockThe status bar is a row of quiet segments on the frame, not a strip. The live agent segment is a lit pill that breathes (2.4s) while a process runs.

Token-level changes

Type
Geist (sans and display) and Geist Mono (code, kbd, figures in meta), both OFL. Fraunces leaves the UI. Weights 400 / 500 / 600 (+650 for match highlights). Display 600 at −0.045 to −0.06em. UI 13–13.5px. Pane titles are 13px/600 in sentence case. Micro-labels are Geist Mono 10.5px at +0.06em (today: Martian 12px at +0.12–0.22em).
Spacing
Island gap 6px; pane head 40px; status bar 30px; tree row 26px; palette row 38px. Control height stays 32px (button, field, select, segmented, icon button: measured below); the small button stays 24px.
Radii
sm 6 / md 8 / lg 14 (today 6 / 10 / 16); island 10; keycap 5; palette 14. No pills on controls.
Surfaces
The neutrals are generated from the accent's hue at near-zero chroma, Linear's three inputs. Dark: canvas oklch L .135 C .006, surface .178, raised .215, tile .255. Light: canvas .928, surface .987, raised 1.0. Text .94 / .79 / .69 on dark, .20 / .38 / .47 on light. On light, the accent used as text is capped at L .46 (the kit uses .50); without it, cyan tags measured 3.97:1.
Borders
Islands are drawn as a 1px white 6.5% ring plus a 1px inset highlight at 7% (dark). The control edge is unchanged: the kit's five-step rule still sets every field, checkbox and secondary button at ≥ 3:1.
Elevation
The page is flat. Overlays (palette, toast) get a four-layer cast: an inset hairline, a black ring at .35, and casts of 8px and 32px. The primary button stands on a 2px darker foot instead of being lit from above (round 1 B used a lit top).
Focus
A flush 2px ring with a 5px glow at 22%. The palette input shows no ring: the palette itself is the focus context.
Motion
Hover 120–140ms (.25,1,.5,1). Press: scale .97 over 220ms (.22,1,.36,1). Islands rise over 560ms, staggered 40–180ms; rows fade in at a 28ms stagger; the toast rises and scales in over 520ms. The palette appears instantly. All motion is off under reduced motion.
Syntax
--sl-code-* is re-bound per theme for the new grounds. The kit's comment colour measured 3.57:1 on its own dark theme; here it is ≥ 4.5.

Adoption cost in the kit

  • sondalab.tokens.json and the generator. Three families and three radii change. The neutrals become oklch(from var(--sl-accent-default) …) formulas instead of hex literals, so gen:tokens must emit relative colours, and sl-audit's value-equality check must accept them. Tier B products that pin literals (daos, introspect) keep theirs.
  • fonts.css + assets/fonts. Vendor Geist and Geist Mono as variable latin woff2 (OFL, two files). The glass lens needs them same-origin anyway. Fraunces stays only for the wordmark and monogram.
  • components.css. About 30 rule overrides: buttons, kbd, fields, check, radio, switch, segmented, chips, badges, tags, list rows, nav items, table, empty, pagination and labels. No class renames and no markup changes, so markup is not affected, but the look changes.
  • New primitives the IDE chrome needs: .sl-pane[data-focus] (island plus lit seam), a keycap .sl-kbd, .sl-toast with a stack, and .sl-statusbar. spexr maps Theia's panels onto .sl-pane in spexr.css; the seam follows Theia's focus tracker.
  • spexr's 32px: not breaking. In spexr, 32px is the kit's control height: icon buttons, and --df-control-height: 2rem on the scheduler and launcher pickers. Lumen keeps every control at 32 (measured). spexr has no tree-row override, so its Theia trees run at Theia's own --theia-content-line-height: 22px. Lumen's 26px tree row would make rows taller if spexr adopted it, never shorter, and it is optional.
  • spexr's indigo fill must widen. spexr-overrides.css (and the registry's fill note) re-bind only .sl-btn--primary to #4c58e9. Every other accent fill that carries a label (count badges, a checked switch's glyph, a highlighted headline) needs the same fill: on #5b6cff white measures 4.17:1. The stage does this in _stage2.css; the kit would add a --slc-accent-fill role.
  • design-language.md. L1 changes: pane titles leave mono uppercase, while micro-labels keep mono at a lower tracking. The serif is reduced to the wordmark.

Risks

  • Generic premium dark. Soft islands are where Linear, Raycast and JetBrains Islands already are. Lumen's owned claim is the lit pane. A first version with only a 1px seam failed the thumbnail test, so the rim and the lighter fill were added. Tone them down in implementation and Lumen becomes a tint of those products.
  • Light theme. The seam has to use the darkened accent-text on near-white, and the light reads weaker. Paper islands depend on the 1px hairline more than the dark ones do.
  • One temperature per product. spexr's ground turns cool indigo and introspect's teal. The family resemblance then rests on shape and type, not on colour.
  • Paint, not border. Rings and casts are box-shadow, so Theia's own widget borders must be zeroed per widget or they double up.
  • FlagNo owner ruling is broken, but the glow ring adds a soft wash outside the flush ring. It is contiguous (no gap), and it is the owner's call whether that still reads as one line.

The markup and the kit classes are the same for the kit and every direction (_screens.js). The difference is one scoped stylesheet, dir-lumen.css, which re-binds --sl-* / --slc-* and overrides component rules. A focus ring is shown statically with .lab-focus, which mirrors :focus-visible.
