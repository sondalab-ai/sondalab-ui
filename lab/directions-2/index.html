Decision

Owner, 2026-10-02.

Build Lumen.

Panes as islands of light with one lit pane, raised-tile selection, keycap shortcuts, sunken fields, a primary standing on its foot, a soft focus glow, a status dock and a toast stack. Geist and Geist Mono carry the voice; the neutrals are tinted by each product's accent.

How it is adopted. The kit is rewritten slice by slice, never by copying this sheet. Each slice is judged on showcase/screens.html, which renders these screens with the kit as it ships and overlays dir-lumen.css as the target.

What stays here. dir-lumen.css is the reference until the last slice removes it. These pages render it on the showcase's own stage (showcase/screens-stage.html), so the reference and the harness cannot drift apart. The design round's own stage, the second direction, the research board and the third-party captures behind both were kept out of the repository.

Everything here lives in lab/directions-2/ and is lab material: nothing in it enters packages/.
