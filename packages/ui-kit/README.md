# @sondalab/ui-kit

Sondalab design tokens as CSS custom properties (`--sl-*`), a CSS-only component layer (`.sl-*`), and an opt-in effects layer (`.sl-fx-*`). No build step; the tokens and components need no runtime JS — import the stylesheets and theme via a `data-sl-theme` attribute. The effects layer ships a small JavaScript driver you call explicitly (see below).

```css
@import "@sondalab/ui-kit/tokens.css";        /* shared: type, spacing, radii, motion, shadow, z */
@import "@sondalab/ui-kit/components.css";     /* .sl-* components (reads role-vars, see below) */
@import "@sondalab/ui-kit/workbench.css";      /* optional: IDE and console chrome, see 0.33 below */
@import "@sondalab/ui-kit/themes/light.css";  /* [data-sl-theme="light"]  surfaces, text, borders, status, accent */
@import "@sondalab/ui-kit/themes/dark.css";
@import "@sondalab/ui-kit/themes/high-contrast.css";
@import "@sondalab/ui-kit/themes/products.css";  /* optional: per-product neutrals, see 0.29 below */
@import "@sondalab/ui-kit/fonts.css";          /* optional: the faces, served from the package, see Fonts below */
```

```html
<html data-sl-theme="dark"> … </html>
```

**Per-product accent.** The bundled accent is the Sondalab default (amber `--sl-accent-default`, teal `--sl-accent-depth`). A product keeps its own signature by overriding the accent tokens after the import:

```css
[data-sl-theme="dark"]  { --sl-accent-default: #2ee6c0; --sl-focus-ring: #2ee6c0; }
[data-sl-theme="light"] { --sl-accent-default: #0e9c84; --sl-focus-ring: #0e9c84; }
```

**Light theme: Prism (0.21).** The light theme's ground was a cool near-white (`#F4F4F8`) until 0.29, when the neutrals became tinted per product (below). It also declares a six-hue spectrum (`--sl-spectrum-0..5`, and a darkened ink set `--sl-spectrum-ink-0..4` for anything seen as text or a dot). The spectrum is not an accent: it is the same in every product and never marks state. Dark and high-contrast reset it to `initial`.

**0.45.0 — the art is on a 2px cell, the posters are procedural prints, and a section can have a horizon.** Three changes to the art, all on the owner's choices of 2026-11-11.

- **Breaking: the posters are new pictures, and two class names are gone.** A poster is a procedural print on its own pastel paper, drawn by code in organic shapes: `sl-art-poster` (a dawn over the sea), `sl-art-poster--overprint` (three inks that cross), `sl-art-poster--hills` (rolling hills and two plants), `sl-art-poster--orbit` (a ringed planet). `--circles` and `--primary` are removed, and the bare poster and `--orbit` show other pictures: the 0.44 Kandinsky paintings are gone, with their bitmap sources. A poster is now one image layer with rounded corners; it has no ink layer.
- **New: `sl-art-horizon`, large art for a section's background.** A scene 200px tall at the section's foot (`--dawn` is the second scene). It has no paper, so the page is its sky; its top dissolves into the page, and the copy stays above it (give the section about `11rem` of bottom padding). The plants of the hills are painted from `--slc-text`.
- **New: `art.js` and `art-core.js`.** `mountArt()` gives a poster with `data-sl-art-seed` its own picture and draws every horizon at its section's real width. It is optional: the stylesheet ships seed 0 of every poster and a static picture of every horizon. `art-core.js` is the pure drawing code.

Every other art piece (the eyebrow glyphs, `sl-rule--shapes`, `sl-rule--weave`, `sl-art-corner`, the four `sl-art-field` tiles and their fades, `sl-empty--void`, `sl-empty--shapes`) was drawn again on 1.5 times the cells, on the owner's choice (2026-11-11, `lab/art-2px`, pick B). Each piece keeps its size in px; its curves and its dither grain are finer. The dither hover, the signal strip and the pixel controls keep their 3px cell. The generated art block is 61,436 bytes (it was 42,998), and its budget rises from 44 KB to 66 KB.

- **Breaking: `--sl-art-mark-cell` counts 2px cells.** The eyebrow glyph is 9 × 6 cells (it was 6 × 4), and the default cell is `2px` (it was `3px`), so the default glyph is still 18 × 12px. A page that sets the property gets a larger glyph than before: `4px` gave 24 × 16px and now gives 36 × 24px. Set `3px` (27 × 18px) for the nearest size.
- **The void's steps moved by a few px.** A stroke slot is 14px (it was 15px) and a stroke is 10 × 2px (it was 9 × 3px). `sl-empty--void` takes its M zone from 392px (it was 390px) and its L zone from 504px (it was 480px).
- **The empty composition's accent ends at 64px** (it was 63px): the disc moved 1px right, onto the 2px grid.

**0.44.0 — the aurora's curtains are the accent and its depth companion.** The ambient light inside a glass pane (`sl-fx-aurora` with `data-sl-fx-live="on"`) paints its two sheets in the accent and in the depth companion, on every theme, and its fade ends at 62% of the sheet, so a card's title and first lines sit on clean glass. Until 0.44 the curtains were the white pole on ink and the text ink on paper, and the fade ran to the top of the pane. The light-theme branch of the curtains is gone. Measured on the showcase card with its amber, indigo and cyan accents, on both themes: the body copy's worst pixel reads 4.87:1 over the light. `--slfx-pillar-count`, the motion and the busy band are unchanged. **The run light's tail is a light on paper too:** `--slfx-light-b` keeps the depth companion's hue with its lightness floored at oklch L 0.76 and its chroma raised by 1.2 up to 0.11, inside sRGB for every registered hue; the companion as declared is dark on the light theme and read as a smear over paper. On ink the tail is nearly what it was. **The art's fades are dissolves:** `sl-art-field--fade-start`, `--fade-end` and `--fade-down` take the field away through the Bayer matrix, so each 3px cell is on or off; until 0.44 they were alpha ramps and the cells turned grey. A dissolve is a band 240px deep from its edge (144px for `--fade-down`); past it the field paints nothing, so a field on a box wider than that is a mark at one edge, where it used to fade across the whole box. **The shapes rule is three shapes:** `sl-rule--shapes` draws a quarter disc, a square and a triangle, 18px each, on its first 90px and then the hairline; it was a 288px strip of twelve 12px shapes that dithered away, and it now reaches 3px further above and below the rule. **The posters are paintings (breaking):** `sl-art-poster` is now a painting by Kandinsky from his Bauhaus years, made to blend into the page: its own ground is transparent, its edges dissolve through the Bayer matrix, and its forms keep their colours in a fixed house palette of 12. Its darkest cells are a mask painted from `--slc-text`, so a black line follows the ground and an inversion band. The four: Composition 8 (the bare poster), `--circles` (Several Circles), `--primary` (Yellow-Red-Blue) and `--orbit` (Circles in a Circle). `--orbit` now names another picture, and `--stair` and `--bleed` are removed, so a page that wears one must change its class. A poster is 126 × 78 cells (a golden rectangle) and draws at 1px per cell in a box under 252px, at 2px from 252px and at 3px from 378px; it was 48 × 32 cells and clipped under 144px. But for its ink it no longer follows the accent or the theme, and it still paints nothing under high contrast or forced colours. The sources are vendored in `scripts/art-sources/` with their origin.  The eight images (a colour layer and an ink layer per poster) cost 14,116 bytes of the generated art block, which is now 42,998 bytes; its budget rises from 32 KB to 44 KB (45,056 bytes). No token or role changes.

**0.43.0 — terminal helpers for Node.** A Node CLI can follow the Term spec (`docs/tui-patterns.md`) without re-deriving it: `@sondalab/ui-kit/term.js` is the JavaScript counterpart of `sondalab-palette` 0.9.0's `term`. Additive: no class, token or role is added, removed or renamed, and no stylesheet changes.

```js
import { colorMode, asciiMode, stepLine, summary, errorBlock, header, needsPrompt, confirm } from "@sondalab/ui-kit/term.js";

const mode = colorMode({ flag: args.color, truecolor: true });   // "none" | "ansi16" | "truecolor"
const ascii = asciiMode({ override: args.ascii || undefined });   // --ascii forces it; omitted, the locale decides
process.stdout.write(header("cld", "0.9.0", "Manage Claude Code loadouts.", { machine: args.json }));
console.log(stepLine("done", "Resolved 128 probes", "2.1s", { ascii, mode }));
console.log(summary(1, 0, 0, 1, { mode, ascii }));
if (needsPrompt("--yes", { given: args.yes, command: "cld rm" }) && !(await confirm("Delete 3 files?"))) process.exit(1);
```

- **The API** mirrors palette 0.9.0 in camelCase: `colorMode`, `asciiMode`, `accessible`, `reducedMotion`, `motionEnabled`, `switchOn`, `paint`, `lockup`, `header`, `stepLine`, `spinnerFrame`, `progressBar`, `decodeFrames`, `summary`, `errorBlock`, `nextLine`, `keyHints`, `arrows`, `heading`, `kvLine`, `width`, `wrap`, `parseYesNo`, `parseChoice`, `needsPrompt`, the errors `TermError`, `NoValidAnswer` and `MissingFlag`, and the async prompts `confirm` and `choose` (stdin and stderr by default, any streams you pass). Keyword arguments become one options object, `isatty` is `isTTY`, `None` is `null`, and a Python `ValueError` or `KeyError` is a `RangeError`. Every `env` defaults to `process.env`, read at call time.
- **Same bytes as Python.** Cuts and widths count code points, prose wraps as `textwrap.fill` does, whitespace and integers (`COLUMNS`, Unicode digits included) parse as Python reads them. `scripts/fixtures/term-parity.json` holds over 600 cases computed by palette-py; Node asserts `term.js` against them and pytest asserts them against `term.py`, so a change on either side fails CI.
- **The constants are generated.** `term-tokens.js` (and its `.d.ts`) is written from `sondalab.tokens.json` by `scripts/gen-term-js.mjs` in `npm run gen:tokens`: `ROLES` (each role's SGR parameters, `ansi16` and `truecolor`), `GLYPHS`, `LAYOUT`, `RAMP`, `SPINNER`, `DECODE`, `PROGRESS`, `MOTION`, `BOX`, `KEYS`, `ROBUSTNESS`, `ENV` and `HEADER`, deep-frozen. `term.js` re-exports them; import them from there, as `term-tokens.js` is not in `exports`.
- **Node only, no dependencies.** ESM, Node 18 or later (`engines`), with types (`term.d.ts`). It imports no CSS and touches no DOM; `node:readline` loads only when a prompt runs. There is no counterpart of `enable_vt()`: Node's console streams handle escape sequences on Windows 10 and later. This is not verified on legacy consoles, and the spec's plain-text fallback has no Node hook; no Windows host was tested. Prompts keep one reader per input stream, so lines piped ahead are answered in turn and a prompt after end of input fails at once; the stream is paused between prompts, so the process can exit.
- **Limits.** Cuts, padding and wrapping count code points, as Python does, not display width: wide (CJK, emoji), combining and ZWJ characters can misalign a column. Without `LANG` or `LC_*` the locale has no charset, so `asciiMode()` is true. `width()` reads `COLUMNS`, then stdout's size, even for text bound for stderr.
- **Consumers.** Pins of `^0.42.x` do not accept 0.43.0; bump explicitly. Nothing changes for a page. `sondalab-palette` is unchanged.

**0.40.0 — the Term spec, drawn.** The command-line surface (the Term substrate) gets a full spec and a place in the catalog. Additive: no class, token or role is removed or renamed.

- **The Term spec.** `docs/tui-patterns.md` grows from the 0.38 patterns into six parts. **Voice**: a running step is a present participle and a finished one past tense; an error is three lines (what, why with the exit code, `Try:` and a command); "did you mean" suggests and never runs; no emoji, "Successfully", exclamation marks or "we". **Motion**: first output within 100ms; the spinner is the dither burst ` ░▒▓█▓▒░`, 8 frames at 100ms in `prompt`, shown only after 300ms and always ending in a step line; a 24-cell progress bar whose leading edge dithers `▓▒░`; at most 10 redraws a second in synchronized output. **Effects**: the glitch becomes the decode, the changed token resolving left to right over 4 frames of 60ms, once, with no inverse or blink. **Pixel text**: the dither ramp `░▒▓█` (ASCII `. : # #`) for the signal strip and progress only, the `sonda/lab` lockup as text, pixel graphics for data behind a capability check. **Full screen**: rounded boxes, the focused pane's title in inverse video, selection as a row tint plus a `▌` seam, dim uppercase headers, one footer of key hints, `[y/N]` for destructive actions and `--plain` for every view. **Robustness**: colour precedence (`--color`/`--no-color`, `NO_COLOR`, `FORCE_COLOR`, TTY), ANSI 16 by default with truecolor on opt-in, OSC 11 with a 100ms timeout, and the env vars `SONDALAB_REDUCED_MOTION`, `SONDALAB_ACCESSIBLE` (and `ACCESSIBLE`), `SONDALAB_ASCII`, `CI` and `TERM=dumb`. In the `design-language.md` matrix the Term column is **A** for L4 motion and for L6 glitch.
- **New `tui` tokens** in `sondalab.tokens.json`: the spinner frames, ASCII frames, interval and delay; the decode glyphs, frames and frame time; the dither ramp; the progress cells and percent width; motion limits; the box set, the seam and the arrow keys; the OSC 11 timeout and prompt retries; the env var names. They reach Python as `sondalab_palette` constants in the companion release, **palette-py 0.6.0**.
- **Terminal role spans** (`workbench.css`). Six spans inside `.sl-term-block__out` paint the six terminal roles from the kit's theme-aware roles, never from the dark-tuned `terminal` truecolor values: `__prompt` the accent as text (the header's prompt class, reused), `__ok` success, `__warn` warning, `__err` danger, `__dim` muted text, `__accent` info (the depth hue is a theme token, not a role). Every role is ≥ 4.5:1 on the block's own fill (raised, or sunken in a lit pane) on every product, theme and band (tested, `workbench.test.mjs`). In a band, dim takes the band's muted share of the band's ink, so it is not a fraction of the output's own secondary ink. Also new: `__inverse` (inverse video, the primary ink on the block's fill), `__sel` (a selected row, a 6% ink wash; the `▌` seam is a `__prompt` span inside it), `__spin` (the spinner, CSS only: a `@keyframes` on its `::before` `content`, one frame per 100ms with `step-end`, only under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`, else the full frame `█`; stopped on that frame under `data-sl-fx-still`) and `.sl-term-block--screen` (no wrap, sideways scroll, the tight line so box strokes meet). The block's font stack ends in a glyph-safe monospace tail (`"SF Mono", Menlo, "DejaVu Sans Mono", Consolas`), since the kit's mono is a latin subset. Forced colours draw the inverse run in `CanvasText` on `Canvas` and the selected row as the system's selection.
- **The catalog's Terminal section** (`agent/catalog.md`, after the terminal block; `catalog.json` indexes it as `term-output`): roles and glyphs, the step list and summary, the error block and "did you mean", the confirmation, progress, the spinner, the decode as a labelled filmstrip of its 4 frames, a full-screen view, and `--plain` and accessible output. Every block's text is the spec's own example (`scripts/term-catalog.test.mjs`), and the showcase's new section 06 draws the same blocks verbatim (the showcase now loads `workbench.css`).
- **Consumers.** Pins of `^0.36.x` and `^0.39.x` do not accept 0.40.0; bump explicitly. sondalab-site parses and renders the Terminal section with the version bump alone; full width on narrow screens needs one small change in its `design-system.astro` (the `ds-spec--wide` condition), which T4 makes. Tokens are added only (`tui`), and palette-py ships them as 0.6.0.

**0.39.0 — the pixel accent (direction D).** The owner picked D from four lab cells (2026-10-26): Lumen stays, and a pixel grid becomes the display accent, the hover and the signal. **Breaking: `.sl-fx-liquid`, `.sl-fx-liquid--drop` and ink (`.sl-fx-ink`) are removed.**

- **The pixel accent.** Geist Pixel Square is vendored (`fonts.css`, latin subset, OFL) as `--sl-font-pixel`, with whole-cell sizes `--sl-text-px-1…4` (12.5 / 25 / 37.5 / 50px: the face draws one cell per 0.08em, so these give 1, 2, 3 and 4px cells). `.sl-stat__value` and the compact lockup (`sonda/lab`, lowercase, the `/` in the accent; `sondalab-lockup.svg` is redrawn to match) use it, and `.sl-px` sets one display word in a heading. The face is proportional with no `tnum`, so a figure that changes in place keeps `--sl-font-mono`. This overturns the 2026-09-13 numeral ruling (L1).
- **The dither hover replaces ink.** A `.sl-btn` or `.sl-icon-btn` answers the pointer with an 8×8 Bayer wipe of 3px cells from where the pointer entered, draining toward where it left (`--sl-motion-dither-in` / `-out`; colours `--slfx-dither-fill` / `--slfx-dither-on`). The layer paints fill cells only, no outline, so a round icon button keeps a smooth circle. On glass it lies **over the tint**: under it, a glass primary's 0.9-alpha accent tint hid the hover almost completely. `dither-core.js` (pure, with types) holds the matrix.
- **The signal strip.** `.sl-signal` is a decorative canvas, a dithered ramp, drawn by `mountSignals()` from `@sondalab/ui-kit/signal.js` in the canvas's own `color`.
- **Live light on the 3px grid.** `live-light.js` samples per 3px cell and cuts the intensity into four steps, on the same lattice as the dither. Reverted in 0.41.1.
- **Pixel switch, check and radio, and a checkerboard for disabled.** The three controls' final frames are drawn by CSS alone on a pixel lattice (3px cells for the switch, 2px for the check and radio marks): a pixel V tick, a 12-cell dot (a 2 x 2 core with an arm on each side), a switch track of 11 × 6 cells with a 12px knob that travels in `steps(5)` (only under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`). The check and radio box keeps its 16px padding box (18px outside); their marks and the dithered fill are 2px cells (`CONTROL_CELL_PX` in `dither-core.js`), the 6 x 6 shapes 12px centred in the box and the switch track 35 × 20px (it was 33.6 × 20), so each holds whole cells. With `mount()` the transition is a canvas over that final frame: the fill dithers in from the box centre (or from the knob) and the tick is plotted cell by cell. New tokens `--sl-motion-check` (180ms), `--sl-motion-radio` (150ms), `--sl-motion-switch` (260ms); the cell lists are exported from `dither-core.js` (`TICK_CELLS`, `DOT_FRAMES`, ...). Every disabled button, icon button, field, chip, segmented item, check, radio and switch is a 50% 1px checkerboard `mask-image` instead of half opacity (high contrast and forced colours keep half opacity; the slider too; a mask flattens a disabled glass button's frost).
- **Tier 2 is retired.** `mountGpu()` stays exported and always resolves `false`.
- **Removed, with a migration hint.**
  - `.sl-fx-liquid` and `.sl-fx-liquid--drop`: delete the class; a round icon button gets the dither hover from `.sl-icon-btn` with nothing to add.
  - `.sl-fx-ink` (the layer `mount()` injected into buttons; there was no class to write) and its `--slfx-ink-*` properties: delete any rule that targets them; `--sl-motion-ink-*` becomes `--sl-motion-dither-in` / `-out`, and the hover is the dither above.
- **Every control rests on a translucent hairline (owner amendment, 2026-10-26).** Text buttons, icon buttons (round too), the field, select and textarea, the check and radio boxes, the switch track, the chip, the segmented track, a button group's outline and rules, the title bar's command field, the page scrollbar's thumb and their glass forms draw `--slc-edge-hairline` at rest: the ink at 21% (dark) or 30% (light), 1.5-2.2:1 on the canvas, the surface and the raised fill, on every product and in a band. - The one control edge (`--slc-edge-control`) steps 5 from the surface toward contrast, plus half a step on a light surface (read from the surface's lightness, so a Tier B layer gets it too; the base is the new `--slc-edge-control-steps`); it reads >= 3.5:1 on the light canvas. - High contrast gains values for the three new roles (tile = raised, sunken = canvas, lit = white) and resets to `initial` every other token light or dark declares that it has no value for (the code palette, the light text cap), so a high-contrast island inherits nothing from the page around it.

### Colour contract — role-vars (`--slc-*`)

Components never read theme tokens directly (those resolve only under `[data-sl-theme]`). They read role-vars that default through a fallback chain to the `--sl-*` token, then to a brand literal. **Tier A** products (full `data-sl-theme`, e.g. spexr, daos) need zero wiring — the role-vars pick up the live theme automatically. **Tier B** products (value-pinned, e.g. introspect) map the role-vars **once** to their own colour layer. That block is the *entire* integration surface:

```css
:root {
  --slc-accent:         var(--your-accent);
  --slc-accent-hover:   var(--your-accent-hover);
  --slc-accent-active:  var(--your-accent-active);
  --slc-accent-subtle:  var(--your-accent-tint);   /* ~12% accent over surface */
  --slc-on-accent:      var(--your-on-accent);     /* text on a filled accent */
  --slc-canvas:         var(--your-canvas);        /* the page ground, below surface */
  --slc-surface:        var(--your-surface);
  --slc-raised:         var(--your-raised);
  --slc-overlay:        var(--your-overlay);        /* dialog backdrop */
  --slc-text:           var(--your-text);
  --slc-text-secondary: var(--your-text-2);
  --slc-text-muted:     var(--your-text-muted);
  --slc-border:         var(--your-border);
  --slc-border-subtle:  var(--your-border-subtle);
  --slc-border-strong:  var(--your-border-strong);
  --slc-focus:          var(--your-accent);
  --slc-success: var(--your-success); --slc-warning: var(--your-warning);
  --slc-danger:  var(--your-danger);  --slc-info:    var(--your-info);
  /* optional (0.31): Lumen's own rungs; unmapped, the tile is your raised,
     the well your surface, the lit line a white 8% hairline */
  --slc-tile:           var(--your-tile);           /* neutrals.json bg-tile */
  --slc-sunken:         var(--your-sunken);         /* neutrals.json bg-sunken */
  --slc-lit:            var(--your-lit);            /* neutrals.json border-lit */
}
```

**Derived roles — never map these.** Two families are computed from the ones above, so a Tier B product gets them in its own colours for free:

- `--slc-accent-text` is the accent as text or a thin line: `oklch(from var(--slc-accent) min(l, var(--sl-accent-text-lmax, 1)) c h)`. On the light theme the accent is a *fill* and the cap (`0.46` since 0.29, `0.5` before) darkens it until it reads at 4.5:1; elsewhere it is the accent. Links, tags, tabs, pressed labels and the focus ring use it. On a solid status fill use `--slc-on-success|warning|danger|info` instead of `--slc-on-accent`: each picks the pole that contrasts with its own fill (white on the light theme, near-black on the dark), where the accent's partner can be dark on dark. Inside `.sl-invert` the ground is the theme's ink — light on the dark and high-contrast themes — so the band re-derives it with its own cap, `--sl-accent-band-text-lmax` (0.45 there, none on light), and a fixed floor (oklch L 0.68) that lifts a dark product hue on the light theme's band. The band also derives `--slc-success-text` / `-warning-` / `-danger-` / `-info-text` (status as text or a thin mark, same floor and cap) and its own translucent `--slc-accent-subtle`; outside a band those four are unset and components fall back to the status role.
- `--slc-edge-*` (`ground`, `surface`, `raised`, `wash`, `accent`, `pressed`, `control`) is each edge as its own fill one step less bright (`--slc-shade-step`, 0.075 of oklch lightness). Every bordered component uses them instead of the neutral `--slc-border*` lines; high contrast maps them back to its drawn border, the inversion band to its re-derived neutrals, except the control edge and its hover, which the band takes from its own ink at a share (0.36.1). Two are not a shade: `pressed` is the control edge itself since 0.36.2 (a pressed toggle's wash in shade read 1.01–1.41:1; high contrast keeps the accent), and in a band `accent` is the fill held to the mid-tones, oklch L 0.55–0.59 (0.36.2).

**Light-layer numbers (Tier B).** The kit sets three numbers only on `:root[data-sl-theme="light"]` and the light theme file, which a `[data-theme]` layer never matches, because they cannot be read off the colours themselves. Set them in your light layer, on the root element (the role-vars resolve there):

```css
:root[data-theme="light"] {
  --sl-accent-text-lmax: 0.46;                    /* the accent as text, capped */
  --slc-edge-accent-lmax: 0.59;                   /* the primary's edge, capped on paper */
  --slc-glow: 0.35;                               /* an accent glow reads at less on paper (0.31) */
}
```

(`--slc-edge-accent-top`, the fourth until 0.31, set the primary's lit top line; the line became a foot in 0.32 and nothing reads it. Drop it.)

**Dark-layer number (Tier B).** An inversion band on the dark theme has a light ground, and the bright accent reads on it at about 1.1:1 until it is capped. The kit's dark and high-contrast theme files set that cap; a `[data-theme]` layer must set it itself. Without it, a seam (0.33) or any accent text inside a dark band falls under 3:1:

```css
:root[data-theme="dark"] {
  --sl-accent-band-text-lmax: 0.45;               /* the accent as text inside an inversion band, capped */
}
```

**An accent that cannot carry its label (0.32).** If neither white nor the dark pole reads at 4.5:1 on your accent, register a deeper `fill` for it in `agent/accent-registry.json`. Since 0.35 a Tier A host needs nothing more: `themes/products.css` sets it as the role `--slc-accent-fill` on the product's theme blocks. A Tier B layer sets the role once, where its theme is set (your root; a subtree works for the kit's controls too), from `neutrals.json` → `products.<id>.fill.<light|dark>`:

```css
[data-theme="light"], [data-theme="dark"] { --slc-accent-fill: var(--your-fill); }   /* neutrals.json products.<id>.fill */
```

The primary, a checked check, radio or switch and any count badge you draw with `var(--slc-accent-fill, var(--slc-accent))` take it, with its label (`--slc-on-accent-fill`), its hover and press (`--slc-accent-fill-hover|active`) and its edge (`--slc-edge-accent`). Unset, all of them are the accent's own. Those public roles compute on `:root`, so a badge of yours follows a root fill only; the kit's controls derive theirs on themselves and follow a fill or an accent set at any scope. Never re-bind `--slc-accent` on a component for this: the accent still drives links, tags, the ring and selection.

The control edge needs nothing: its extra half step on a light surface is read from the surface. Your neutrals (`--your-canvas`, `--your-text`, the borders, the overlay) come from `neutrals.json` → `products.<id>.<light|dark>`.

**Inversion bands.** `.sl-invert` ships the fix. Inside a band whose background is the strongest neutral, `--slc-border`'s alpha-over-canvas value sits on the wrong ground and the hairline disappears — and a component that paints its own surface keeps the light one under re-derived light text. The class re-derives the border, text **and surface** role-vars from the band's own foreground, on its children, so any component placed inside keeps a visible edge and a ground it can be read against. A product rolling its own band does the same by hand:

```css
.invert > * {
  --slc-border:  color-mix(in srgb, currentColor 22%, transparent);
  --slc-surface: color-mix(in srgb, currentColor 7%, transparent);
}
```

The re-derivation sits on the children, not on the band: a custom property whose value references one declared on the same element is a cycle, and CSS makes every property in a cycle invalid — the band would paint itself its own foreground. Since 0.36.1 the class also binds the band's control edge, the band's ink at the least share that reads 3:1 there; write it on the band's ink rather than `currentColor`, which a control in a lighter ink (a chip, an icon button) would dilute.

The full reference implementation — every component in all three themes — is `showcase/index.html` (§04).

## Effects (`effects.css` + `effects.js`, opt-in)

Four additive families, each carrying one meaning, composed onto a shipped `.sl-*` class — never replacing it. **Glass** (`sl-fx-glass`) — the element's own border as chrome; with `--pane`, the element is made of glass: a slab of light under the label, a glint that follows the pointer, a hairline that turns to face it with a fringe of colour either side. A glass text button takes `--pane`, whatever its variant, and the material owns its depth (0.32: the primary's foot, the secondary's cast and a field's well stand down, and the glass edge carries the focus halo); with `--text` the letterform itself is the pane — a mask cuts the glass to the element's own glyphs, measured off the element, so the type stays entirely the surface's. **Aurora** (`sl-fx-aurora`) — a 1px arc of light riding the border while attention lives on the element: focus, `data-sl-fx-live="on"` (one ambient sweep per document), `data-sl-fx-live="busy"` (with `aria-busy`), or a determinate arc from an inline `--slfx-progress` (with `role="progressbar"`). **Goo** (`sl-fx-goo` on a container + `sl-fx-goo__member` on each element in the set — standard elements only; `button`, `input`, `select`, `textarea` and `a[href]` are refused) — several elements read as one substance, welding where they come within `--slfx-goo-weld` of one another; it answers the layout, not the pointer, and each member's silhouette is painted in that member's own fill so their colours blend across the neck. **Glitch** (`sl-fx-glitch`) — a finite burst on a child `<span>`, fired from JavaScript.

```css
@import "@sondalab/ui-kit/effects.css";   /* after components.css */
@import "@sondalab/ui-kit/compositions.css"; /* optional, AFTER effects.css — bindings only */
```

**Compositions.** `compositions.css` is an optional stylesheet carrying the custom-property bindings a composition needs: today, the pane-swell stand-down on `.sl-figure__media`, where a pane filling the media box would have its 3% hover swell and its seated light cut by the wrapper's `overflow: hidden`. It contains no paint and no new class, and is inert without `effects.css`. The canonical class string per component lives in `agent/catalog.md` § Effects.

```js
import { mount, mountGpu, burst, autoBurst, hoverBurst, scramble, grantLive } from "@sondalab/ui-kit/effects.js";
mount();                      // Tier 1: the goo's fields, glitch bursts, the aurora's sweep. Explicit, never automatic.
// A product with a documented reason to animate for a visitor reporting
// prefers-reduced-motion takes the derogation explicitly. It reaches the
// GLITCH and the HALFTONE's motion; high contrast still refuses.
// mount(document, { ignoreReducedMotion: true });
// bursts: the ambient glitch ceiling, default 1. A page with several ambient
// glitches raises it, or most of them are dropped.
// mount(document, { bursts: 3 });
//
// burst(el)                     — one finite burst

mountGpu().then((ok) => {});  // Tier 2 is retired: always resolves false (kept for existing callers).

//   .folio-slot { display: inline-grid; }

// One line only: the mask is a single SVG <text> run and SVG text does not wrap.
```

- **Tiers.** Without `mount()` the CSS alone is Tier 0 and correct on its own: a static glass hairline, a static aurora arc on focus, and a goo field's members simply laid out unmerged. `mount()` arms Tier 1 by setting `data-sl-fx="on"` on `<html>`. `mountGpu()` needs `vgpu` (an optional peer dependency — install it yourself if you want Tier 2; nothing breaks without it) and sets `data-sl-fx-gpu="on"` only once a frame is actually on screen.
- **Kill switches, all honoured at every tier:** `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` (nothing animates; `mount()` refuses), `[data-sl-theme="high-contrast"]` (every family off, the GPU tier torn down), and `data-sl-fx="off"` on any element (that element and its subtree). The focus `outline` is never replaced or removed.
- **Holding still:** set `data-sl-fx-still` on `<html>` while the app is idle or its window is unfocused. The attribute works by presence: any value holds, `"false"` included, so remove it to resume. Motion freezes in place and resumes where it stopped when the attribute is removed: the live light (`live-light.js`) draws the frame it is on and asks for no more, keeping its canvases and WebGL contexts; the halftone (`halftone.js`) freezes on the frame it is on and resumes from it; and every kit animation (aurora sweep, curtains, beam, busy/run orbit, goo drift, the live badge's breath) is paused with `animation-play-state`. A glitch stands down to its label instead of freezing mid-burst. It is a pause, not a kill switch: nothing is detached, and apart from the glitch nothing changes how it looks. Not reached: the pointer-driven loops in `effects.js` (specular, press), which run only while the pointer moves; the glitch's `autoBurst` timers, which keep starting bursts that the flag then takes off; a drifting goo field's `goo-field.js` measuring loop; and anything the product animates itself, including animations it puts on a glass or aurora host.
- **Replaced controls** (`<input>`, `<textarea>`, `<select>`) render no pseudo-element: wrap them in `<span class="sl-field__control sl-fx-…">` and put the effect classes on the wrapper.
- **Filled elements** (`sl-btn--primary`) take `sl-fx-aurora--rim`, which steps the arc just outside the fill — and tells a glass pane that its ground is the fill.

**Knobs a surface is expected to set.** Everything else is derived; these four are the ones written by hand.

| Property | On | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--slfx-pillar-count` | an aurora host | `3` | how many curtains cross the host. The intensity control: fewer bands are wider bands. The pitch follows from the host's own width, so the pattern scales with the element rather than holding a fixed pixel size. |
| `--slfx-pane-swell` | a glass pane, **or any ancestor of it** | `1.03` | on a control pane it scales the kit's press (k = (swell − 1) / 0.03): an icon button's, chip's, segmented or pagination item's squash, a text button's 97% sink (0.36); on any other pane it drives the uniform press sink, a third of the swell the other way (0.99 at the default). `1` stands either down. Since 0.36 it also governs the press of a bare `.sl-btn` and an `sl-fx-press` button, glass or not, under any ancestor that sets it: for example, spexr's `.spexr-whats-new.sl-fx-glass { --slfx-pane-swell: 1 }` keeps its dismiss button from sinking (its glass pane press was already muted there), and a bare `.sl-btn` inside `.sl-figure__media` no longer sinks where `compositions.css` is loaded; `.sl-btn-group` binds it to `1` in `components.css`. The hover growth this used to drive retired in 0.13 with `sl-fx-press`. Set it to `1` on a wrapper that scales the pane itself — otherwise the two scales compose and the pane's seated light spills past its edge. |
| `--slg-lens-disperse` | a lens host | `18` | the lens's red-to-blue separation, in px. Not raised in the default because it applies to everything behind the pane — at 60 the page's own text splits into visible RGB copies — so raise it per surface. |
| `--slg-lens-reach` | a lens host | `6` (`18` on an aurora host) | how far the lens is allowed to bend. The box caps it too, at a sixth of its shorter side, so a 32px control bends at most ~5.3px whatever this asks; a lens nested inside another glass pane is refused and keeps the material. |
| `--slg-lens-bulge` | a lens host | `0.4` | the pane's convex bulge: magnification at its centre as a fraction, falling to nothing at the rim; `0` turns it off. The owner's pick, 2026-09-25. |
| `--slfx-text-light` | a `--text` host or any ancestor | `--slc-text` | the light the cut glyphs are lit by, with `--slfx-text-core-a` (46%) and `--slfx-text-halo-a` (26%) for the two shadows. Rebind all three inside an inversion band, where `--slc-text` is the band's own ground. |

Read every one of them with `var(--name, default)` at the point of use if you extend this layer: a default **declared** on the pane or the glow would shadow the value an ancestor inherits down, and be unreachable from there.

### Halftone — `data-sl-halftone` (0.19.0)

A picture drawn in round dots whose size follows its light, inside a circle that dissolves at its edge — sondalab.eu's stance and method vignettes. The dots gather the first time the canvas is 40% in view, then float. The ink is the canvas's own `color`: dark ink prints the picture's dark (a halftone on paper), light ink its light. Armed by `mount()`, or by `halftone(root)` from `@sondalab/ui-kit/halftone.js` without it. Under reduced motion the settled picture is drawn, still; `data-sl-fx-still` holds it; high contrast hides it.

```html
<canvas data-sl-halftone data-sl-src="/art/kinship.png" data-sl-grid="130" aria-hidden="true"
        style="width: 22rem; aspect-ratio: 1"></canvas>
```

`data-sl-grid` is cells per side (16–256, default 96); `data-sl-seed` fixes which edge dots survive and how they scatter. A non-square picture is centre-cropped. **What reads as dots:** one iconic subject with near-binary contrast, drawn at ≥ 320px with a grid near 130; busy photos and dark renders do not. The engine is `@sondalab/ui-kit/halftone-core.js` (`sampleDots`, `dotFrame`, `settledFrame`, `toSVG`, `cover`), and `tools/halftone/` in the repo turns any picture into an SVG or PNG with the same dots.

Generated tokens (`tokens.css`, `themes/*.css`) come from the canonical `sondalab.tokens.json` — do not hand-edit them. `components.css` is hand-authored.
