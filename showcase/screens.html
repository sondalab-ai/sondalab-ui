Showcase · the kit in real product screens

Real screens, today's kit.

Four product screens (the spexr IDE, a settings page, a data view and the sondalab.eu hero) built from the kit's own classes, one theme per frame. Every view measures itself: text contrast, the contrast of each control's boundary, and control heights. Target: Lumen overlays the direction the kit is moving to, in place, so each slice can be judged against it. Press T to flip.

– measuring

Measured in each frame

WCAG 2 contrast of every text role the screen shows against its composited ground (≥ 4.5 at any size), the contrast of each control's boundary against what surrounds it (≥ 3), and control heights against the kit's 32px. Rows marked · are information, not checks.

The stage is screens-stage.html + screens.css + screens.js, ported from lab/directions-2/. The kit view loads only the kit's stylesheets and its own fonts.css. The target is lab/directions-2/dir-lumen.css, scoped by html[data-dir="lumen"], with Geist and Geist Mono from Google Fonts until K3 vendors them; it is a reference, never copied into packages/. Serve the repository with python3 lab/_serve.py: the plain http.server lets Chrome keep a stale stylesheet across reloads. The lab's own side-by-side is Kit vs Lumen.
