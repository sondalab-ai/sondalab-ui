sondalab.

Built for operators, not passengers.

Sondalab builds instruments you operate — local, deterministic, observable. Not a black box you defer to; a panel with the controls in reach. You send a signal, you read what comes back, you decide what's next.

The throughline

Every tool works the same way: you send a signal, you read what comes back, you stay in charge. No autopilot, no black box. Sondalab is that instinct as a toolkit — instruments you operate, not systems you defer to.

The suite already speaks one language: daos makes agent output deterministic and signed; introspect shows what Claude is doing right now; smartctx probes context before it loads; local-agents routes work across models you own.

The brand names the throughline — instruments you operate, run where you choose, that keep you in command of what the machine does.
